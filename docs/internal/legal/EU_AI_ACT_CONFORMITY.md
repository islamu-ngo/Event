# EU AI Act Conformity Declaration & Technical Documentation

> **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council**  
> Scope: ISLAMU Event Autonomous Agentic & AI Assistant Infrastructure  
> Last Updated: 2026-08-22

---

## 1. System Risk Classification & Scope

Under the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act, Regulation 2024/1689), the AI capabilities integrated into the ISLAMU Event platform are classified as follows:

| Component / Subsystem | System Classification | Applicable EU AI Act Articles |
|---|---|---|
| **AI Assistant / Assistant Gateway** | General Purpose AI & Transparency Risk | Article 50 (Transparency obligations for providers and deployers) |
| **Agentic Engineering Subagents** | Internal Developer Tooling / Automation | Articles 4, 12, 14 (Technical documentation, logging, oversight) |
| **Search & Discovery Recommendation** | Minimal / Low Risk | Article 50 (Transparency) |
| **High-Criticality Business Handlers** | Zero-AI Autonomous Execution (Pure Deterministic C#) | Excluded from High-Risk Annex III (Human/Deterministic Domain) |

---

## 2. Article 50 Transparency Compliance

In accordance with **Article 50(1) & Article 50(2)**:

1. **AI Output Disclosure**: All user-facing AI responses generated through `Explore.Application.Features.AiAssistant` include an explicit machine-readable metadata header (`X-AI-Generated: true`) and UI disclosure badges informing users that content was generated or assisted by artificial intelligence.
2. **Context Disclosure & Consent**: AI model context is strictly governed by `IAiContextGateway` and the `AiContextDisclosureRegistry`. No tenant or user data is submitted to an external foundation model provider without prior explicit tenant consent (`AiConsentGrant`).
3. **Deepfake / Synthetic Content Fencing**: Synthetic images, transcripts, or AI summaries generated by platform tools are cryptographically tagged or watermarked with provenance metadata in adherence to Article 50(4).

## 3. Annex IV Technical Documentation Matrix

In accordance with **Annex IV (Technical documentation referred to in Article 11)**:

### 3.1. General Description of the AI System
- **System Architecture**: Orchestrated multi-agent architecture utilizing foundation LLMs via isolated MCP (Model Context Protocol) and native CQS command handlers.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Developer automation, code quality verification, multi-language localization assistance, and attendee FAQ summarization. The AI system is explicitly forbidden from making autonomous financial settlement, tax determination, or identity revocation decisions.

### 3.2. Data Governance & Training Data Transparency
- **Zero Training on User Data**: The platform does not use tenant event data, registration answers, or attendee PII for foundation model training or weight fine-tuning.
- **Clean-Room Provenance**: External AI inputs, generated code artifacts, and third-party prompts are governed under `docs/legal/IP_GOVERNANCE.md` and clean-room functional specifications.

### 3.3. Logging, Auditability, and Technical Record-Keeping (Article 12)
- **Deterministic Redaction**: Telemetry pipelines leverage `Microsoft.Extensions.Compliance.Redaction` (`StarRedactor` and `HmacRedactor`) ensuring that prompt evaluation logs, traces, and metrics contain zero plaintext PII or unmasked user secrets.
- **Traceability**: All agent interactions, intent classifications, and model responses are logged with correlation IDs, model versions, prompt hash fingerprints, and token usage metrics.

### 3.4. Human Oversight & Intervention (Article 14)
- **Stop Conditions & Approval Gates**: All high-criticality actions (Tier 0 Sovereign Financial mutations, Tier 1 Security policy modifications, and Tier 2 Privacy erasures) require explicit, non-bypassable human confirmation or deterministic architecture test gates before production application.
- **Kill-Switch Capabilities**: Every AI background worker and MCP service supports immediate runtime cancellation via ASP.NET Core `CancellationToken` and Aspire orchestration state management.
