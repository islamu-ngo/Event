# Individual event-resource certificates

- Owner: Admission and product.
- Activate when: Personalized award issuance has an approved participation and correction policy.

## Acceptance

- Bind each certificate to the actual recipient's current identity and completion authority; a shared event-resource grant cannot expose another person's certificate.
- Handle correction, replacement, revocation and erasure without resurrecting old credentials or leaking identity in generic resource discovery.
- Document issuance/audit purpose and prove tenant isolation, replay and denied access after a completion reversal.
