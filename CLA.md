# ISLAMU Contributor License Agreement

The single authoritative Contributor License Agreement—used identically across ISLAMU's AGPLv3 self-hostable platforms—is maintained at [`legal/CLA.md`](legal/CLA.md).

Every non-bot contributor must sign [`legal/CLA.md`](legal/CLA.md) before a pull request can be merged (or provide signed written/email confirmation accepted by the Project Steward for offline or historical contributions).

## How to Sign

Post a comment on your pull request with the exact text:

```text
I have read and agree to the ISLAMU Contributor License Agreement v1.0, and I confirm that I have the right to submit my contribution under it.
```

To re-run the CLA check after signing, post a comment containing only `recheck`.
