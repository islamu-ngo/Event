# Event-resource guest and dependent access

- Owner: Identity and privacy.
- Activate when: A supported guest, dependent or guardian participation workflow is selected for event materials.

## Acceptance

- Introduce purpose-bound capabilities tied to the actual participant and, where justified, guardian authority; a purchaser or broad identity claim never inherits that access.
- Prove expiry, replay, transfer, revocation, tenant switch and erasure with direct authorization tests, including an old link after a grant changes.
- Record only necessary authorization evidence with zero-PII telemetry. Do not grant guest access from guessed email or a general tenant membership.
