## Why A CLA Alongside Open-Source Licensing?

Each Covered Project is publicly distributed as a complete application under its declared open-source license (such as `AGPL-3.0-or-later` for ISLAMU's self-hostable platforms). That public license remains in place for the Covered Project as a whole and for anyone who receives it under that license.

This CLA is an additional inbound license from contributors to the ISLAMU Project Steward. It serves two bounded purposes:

1. **Alternative Non-SaaS Licensing:** It allows the ISLAMU Project Steward to license copyleft Covered Projects under bilateral alternative terms (paid or gratis; see [`ISLAMU-organizational-use-license.txt`](ISLAMU-organizational-use-license.txt)) to any recipient seeking terms other than `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, strictly limited to the recipient's own organizational use and subject to the Anti-SaaS Covenant below.
2. **Cross-Project Open-Source Reuse Across ISLAMU Projects:** It allows ISLAMU to extract, adapt, and reuse relevant portions of Contributions inside any present or future open-source software project, library, utility, tool, template, or specification stewarded by ISLAMU under any Open Source Initiative (OSI)–approved open-source license meeting the MIT-level notice and disclaimer floor below (never dedicating code to the public domain). Where ISLAMU incorporates a portion of a Contribution into another ISLAMU repository licensed under a permissive license such as `MIT`, recipients of that other repository receive that incorporated portion under its respective license, while each copyleft Covered Project itself remains under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.

The CLA does not take ownership away from contributors. Contributors keep ownership of their own Contributions. The CLA gives the Project Steward the rights needed to maintain, distribute, sublicense, relicense, share across ISLAMU's open-source ecosystem, and protect Covered Projects sustainably.

### Community Protection, Cross-Project Boundaries & The Anti-SaaS Covenant

The Project Steward is bound by strict community stewardship principles governing both Anti-SaaS protection and cross-project open-source reuse:

1. **Copyleft Platforms Remain Free & Open Source:** Each Covered Project distributed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` remains free and open source as a complete product under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` (and every other Covered Project remains free and open source under its declared open-source license).
2. **Organizational-Use Only (Anti-SaaS Covenant for Alternative Licenses):** No entity may use a bilateral alternative license from ISLAMU to operate a closed-source Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) or managed cloud platform for third parties that denies source code back to the community. Any bilateral alternative license granted by the Project Steward for a Covered Project (whether for a fee or gratis) is strictly restricted to **operating the software—whether self-managed or on privately dedicated infrastructure—solely for the licensee organization's own operations and end-user services** (including permitting the organization's own external members, visitors, or users to interact with that organization's self-hosted instance). It waives AGPL copyleft contagion over the licensee's internal integrations and operations, while expressly prohibiting reselling or offering the software as a multi-tenant SaaS, hosted platform, or managed service for third-party organizations.
3. **Universal SaaS Parity:** Any party wishing to offer an AGPL-licensed Covered Project as a public, multi-tenant, or commercial SaaS to third-party organizations must do so under the public **`AGPL-3.0-or-later`** license, guaranteeing that all SaaS modifications are shared back with the community.
4. **Cross-Project Open-Source License Floor (OSI-Approved, MIT Minimum — No Public Domain):** When incorporating, extracting, or distributing portions of Contributions within other open-source projects stewarded by ISLAMU, the Project Steward may select any license approved by the Open Source Initiative (OSI) as an Open Source License (including, by way of non-exhaustive example, `MIT`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `ISC`, `Apache-2.0`, `EUPL-1.2`, `MPL-2.0`, `LGPL-3.0-or-later`, `GPL-3.0-or-later`, and `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, or subsequent versions thereof), strictly subject to:
   - **(a) Minimum Notice & Disclaimer Floor:** No Contribution or portion thereof may be dedicated to the public domain or distributed under zero-condition or notice-waiving terms (explicitly excluding `CC0-1.0`, `Unlicense`, `WTFPL`, `0BSD`, and `MIT-0`). Every outbound open-source license selected by the Project Steward must require retention of copyright notices, permission/license notices, and warranty/liability disclaimers at least as protective as the `MIT` License.
   - **(b) Distinct Projects Only (No Wholesale Permissive Re-Release of Copyleft Platforms):** Cross-project permissive licensing applies solely to relevant code, libraries, modules, tooling, specifications, or components reused in distinct ISLAMU open-source projects—never to republishing the entirety (or substantially all) of any AGPL-licensed Covered Project under a permissive non-copyleft license.

## How To Sign

Every non-bot contributor to a pull request must sign this CLA by posting the following exact comment on the pull request:

```text
I have read and agree to the ISLAMU Contributor License Agreement v1.0, and I confirm that I have the right to submit my contribution under it.
```

An automated CLA verification workflow records each pull-request signature in the repository's signature archive (currently `signatures/v1.0/cla.json` on the dedicated `cla-signatures` branch), storing the contributor's platform username, account ID, pull request number, signing comment ID and body, and an ISO-8601 timestamp.

If the CLA version changes due to a material revision of licensing terms or contributor rights, signatures are recorded under the new version segment and contributors sign the updated version. Corporate lifecycle milestones—including the Organization acquiring legal personality and ratifying this Agreement pursuant to Article 2:2 CSA—operate strictly within Version 1.0, do not change the signature path, and do not require re-signing.

To re-run the CLA check after signing, post a comment containing only `recheck`. Where agreed with the Project Steward (such as for offline or pre-existing historical contributors), a signed written or email confirmation attaching or referencing this Agreement is likewise retained as binding acceptance.
