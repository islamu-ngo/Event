# Event-resource templates and federation

- Owner: Domain and federation.
- Activate when: A reviewed user-visible template or federated-resource projection is required.

## Acceptance

- Templates may carry draft semantic placeholders only, never cloned attachment ownership, private title or a protected destination.
- Announcements expose authoritative-instance links only after explicit consent and a fresh disclosure decision; a copied event does not inherit source-tenant access.
- Exercise populated event/resource projections through template, search, AI and federation consumers with cross-tenant and revoked-reader cases.
