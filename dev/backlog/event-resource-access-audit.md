# Identified event-resource access audit

- Owner: Privacy steward.
- Activate when: A specific incident-handling need justifies identified access history.

## Acceptance

- Document necessity, purpose, legal basis, authorized readers and short retention before adding any event, log or dashboard.
- Exercise subject erasure/export, tenant isolation and access to audit records; never log URLs, tokens, storage keys, full titles, IPs or user agents by default.
- Preserve the v1 baseline of no identified attendee browsing history or attendance-surveillance feed.
